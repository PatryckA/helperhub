Rostering helpers, minus the ads.

HelperHub is a free volunteer sign-up sheet for P&Cs, sports clubs and community groups. Parents pick a time slot or something to bring in under a minute, and nobody has to create an account.

Set up your group, free Organiser login

No ads, no accounts

Volunteers just enter their name, email and mobile. No passwords, no app, no clutter.

Shifts and bring-a-plate

Time slots for canteen or BBQ duty, plus "things to bring" with dietary tags so you don't get five lasagnes.

Reminders sent for you

Confirmation emails with a calendar invite, and a reminder before each shift. Volunteers can cancel with one tap.

Your colours, your logo

Your group gets its own page, like helperhub.au/your-club, with your logo and colours.

Built for busy committees

Copy last year's event in one click, print a sign-in sheet, and add as many organisers as you need.

Private by default

Other parents see only a first name and initial. Contact details are for your organisers only.

How it works

  1. Set up your group with a name and web address. Takes two minutes.
  2. Add an event with time slots or items to bring.
  3. Share the link in your newsletter, class chat or team group.
  4. Relax. HelperHub sends confirmations and reminders, and you can see who's coming at a glance.
